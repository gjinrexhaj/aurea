Body Text

Body text — The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Aurea prioritizes legibility with antialiased rendering and optimizeLegibility text rendering.

Small body text — Used for descriptions and secondary content in instrument cards and panels.

Caption text — Monospace captions in gallery tiles and figure descriptions.

Faint text — Metadata labels and timestamps, set in monospace.
